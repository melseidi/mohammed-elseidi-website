Research

Decompose first, then learn: hybrid frameworks for time series with complex seasonality

1Observed series data with two seasonal cycles
2Decomposition (MSTL / STR) trendseasonalremainder
3Neural network on the adjusted series, then recombine forecast80 / 95% intervals

Why it works: once the seasonal cycles are stripped out, what remains is far easier for a neural network (NNAR or N-BEATS) to learn, and the cycles themselves are extrapolated by the decomposition. The illustration uses simulated data; run the real models on your own series in the Forecast Lab.

Seasonal-trend decomposition

Methods for series with several overlapping cycles: multiple STL, regression-based STR with evolving seasonal shapes, and a quantile-regression approach to seasonal adjustment that is robust to outliers and heavy tails.

MSTLSTRQuantile regressionComplex seasonality

Hybrid statistical and machine-learning forecasting

Frameworks that pair a decomposition with a learner suited to what is left: MSTL-NNAR (neural network autoregression), STR-NBEATS (deep residual stacks) and Prophet-ARIMA. Each is benchmarked on hold-out data against classical baselines.

Presentation Title

Forecast Lab

Run my published forecasting models on your own data, free and without an account

Upload a time series. Get a forecast, intervals and diagnostics.

Both of my hybrid frameworks are implemented end to end in JavaScript and run entirely inside your web browser. Drop in a CSV, choose a model, and the decomposition and the neural network are fitted on your own machine.

  • Nothing is uploaded. Your data never leaves your device — there is no server to send it to.
  • Full output. Point forecasts with prediction intervals, seasonal-trend decomposition, residual diagnostics and a downloadable CSV.
  • Honest evaluation. Every run is scored on a hold-out sample against a seasonal-naive benchmark.
  • Built for teaching. Automatic seasonality detection and example series make it a ready-made classroom demonstration.
